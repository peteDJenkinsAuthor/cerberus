Cerberus

Password Manager

A password and secrets manager that never leaves your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play

Download the beta   See all features

The beta is a limited test version. Read the notes first.

Local only

No account, no cloud, no analytics, and no internet permission. Your vault lives in the app's private storage.

Strong encryption

AES-256-GCM with a random vault key. Your master password is stretched with Argon2id, tuned to your phone, and you also get a 20-word recovery phrase and a recovery image.

More than logins

Logins, cards, identities, bank accounts, passkeys, bookmarks (including .onion), API secrets, crypto wallets, contacts and secret notes.

Built-in authenticator

Time-based one-time codes with next-code preview, groups and custom icons.

Autofill

Fill logins, cards and details in apps and browsers, and save new ones, after you unlock with your PIN, fingerprint or master password.

Generator

Passwords, passphrases and usernames with color-coded output.
