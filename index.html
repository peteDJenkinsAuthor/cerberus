Cerberus

A password and secrets manager that never leaves your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play

Local only

No account, no cloud, no analytics, and no internet permission. Your vault lives in the app's private storage.

Strong encryption

AES-256-GCM with keys derived by Argon2id from two master passwords, plus a 20-word recovery phrase and a recovery image.

More than logins

Logins, cards, identities, bank accounts, passkey notes, bookmarks (including .onion), API secrets, contacts and secret notes.

Built-in authenticator

Time-based one-time codes with next-code preview, groups and custom icons.

Autofill

Fill logins, cards and details in apps and browsers, and save new ones, after you unlock with a PIN and fingerprint, or your master password.

Generator

Passwords, passphrases and usernames with color-coded output.
