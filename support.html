Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback: ghosted.technologies@proton.me

I lost a way in

On the unlock screen choose Other ways in. Any master password together with your recovery phrase works, as do your two master passwords, or both master passwords with your recovery image. Cerberus is local-only and we hold no copy of your data or keys, so if both the master password and the recovery phrase are lost, the vault cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Moving to a new phone

Export your vault from Settings, move the file yourself, and import it on the new device. Cloud backup is turned off on purpose. Treat export files as unencrypted and delete them afterwards.

Turning on autofill

In Cerberus open Settings, then Autofill, and choose Cerberus as your autofill service. You can pick how suggestions appear and turn "Ask to save" on or off there.

Importing from another password manager

Cerberus can import a Bitwarden export. Choose the file under Settings, Vault.

Reporting a security issue

Email ghosted.technologies@proton.me with the details. Please give us a reasonable time to fix a problem before sharing it publicly.
