I forgot my master password

Use the recovery option on the unlock screen. You will need your 20-word recovery phrase and your recovery image together; neither one works alone. After they check out, you choose a new master password and your vault is opened with it.

A PIN or fingerprint may still open the app for a while, but Cerberus asks for the master password again after a restart, after the screen has been off a long time, and at least once a day by default (you can change this in Settings). It also asks again for sensitive actions such as exports and security settings. While it still opens, make an encrypted backup (no master password needed; it will need your recovery phrase to open). Do not wait for that moment to find out you cannot recover.

Cerberus is local-only and we hold no copy of your data or keys. If you lose your master password and either part of your recovery (the phrase or the image), the vault cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Typing your recovery phrase

Enter all 20 words in order. Spaces, hyphens, dots, underscores, line breaks, numbering such as “1.”, capital letters and other punctuation between the words are all ignored, so you can type the words one after another or paste the one-line version from your phrase file. The screen counts your words and marks any word that is not in the word list.

Using your recovery image

Use the exact PNG file you saved from Cerberus. It only works as that original file. A screenshot, a crop, a resize, a conversion to JPEG, or sending it through a messaging app or photo service that recompresses pictures will break it. After saving it, use Test in Settings, Security to confirm it works, and keep copies of the original file in more than one safe place.

Moving to a new phone

Export your vault from Settings, move the file yourself, and import it on the new device. Cloud backup is turned off on purpose. Treat export files as unencrypted and delete them afterwards. Encrypted backups (.cerb files) are protected, and you can make them also require your recovery phrase.

Turning on autofill

In Cerberus open Settings, then Autofill, and choose Cerberus as your autofill service. You can pick how suggestions appear and turn “Ask to save” on or off there.

Importing from another password manager

Cerberus can import a Bitwarden export. Choose the file under Settings, Vault.

Reporting a security issue

Email ghosted.technologies@proton.me with the details. Please give us a reasonable time to fix a problem before sharing it publicly.
