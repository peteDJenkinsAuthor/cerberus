Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Summary

Cerberus Password Manager ("Cerberus", "the app") is published by ghosted.technologies ("we", "us"). Cerberus is a local-only password and secrets manager. Your vault stays on your device. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal information, and the app has no internet access of its own.

Information we collect

None. Cerberus has no accounts or sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting service. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data off your device on its own.

Data you store in the app

Everything you enter (logins, cards, identities, bank details, crypto wallet details, notes, bookmarks, API secrets, passkeys, authenticator keys and custom fields) is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and saved only in the app's private storage on your device. The keys are protected by your master password, which is stretched with Argon2id. We cannot read your data and cannot recover it. If you lose your master password and your recovery phrase or recovery image, your data cannot be recovered by anyone.

Master password, PIN and biometrics

Cerberus uses one master password. The app also asks for it again from time to time, for example after a restart or a long period with the screen off, and for sensitive actions. If you choose, you can unlock between those times with a PIN or your fingerprint. A PIN is combined with a key that never leaves your phone's secure hardware and is never stored in readable form. Fingerprint or face unlock is performed by Android; Cerberus never receives or stores your biometric data and only receives a yes or no from the system. The app keeps a few small settings on your device to do this, such as when you last entered your master password and how many wrong tries have been made. None of it is sent anywhere.

Clipboard

When you copy a password, code or other value, it is placed on the Android clipboard and cleared automatically after the delay you choose in Settings. While it is on the clipboard, other apps on your device may be able to read it, depending on your Android version.

Autofill

If you turn on Cerberus as your autofill service, Android tells Cerberus which app or website is asking for a login and which fields are on screen. This is used only on your device to find matching items. It is not stored and not transmitted. Nothing is filled until you unlock the vault and choose an item. If "Ask to save" is on, then after you enter a new password, card or contact details in another app or browser, Android may offer to save them to Cerberus. What you typed is passed to Cerberus on your device only, and is stored in your encrypted vault only if you confirm. You can turn this off in Settings > Autofill.

Backups, exports and imports

Android cloud backup of the app's data is turned off. If you export your vault, or import a file from another password manager, that file is created or read only where you choose. You are responsible for protecting files you export or import. A file imported from another manager may be unencrypted. Encrypted backups (.cerb files) are protected by a password you choose, and you can also make them require your recovery phrase; we cannot recover that password or open the file. If you turn on personal sync, Cerberus writes an encrypted file for each of your devices into a folder that you pick. Cerberus has no network access; if that folder is kept in step by another app or service (for example Syncthing, Nextcloud or a cloud drive), that app and its provider handle the file under their own terms and can see its name, size and timestamps, but not its contents.

Third parties

Cerberus contains no third-party tracking or advertising code. It uses open-source libraries for the interface and encryption, listed under Open-source licenses; none of them transmit your data. If you install Cerberus through Google Play, Google collects its own information under Google's terms and privacy policy. If we offer paid features, payment is processed by Google Play; we do not see your payment details.

Future features

If a future feature needs to connect to a network, we will update this policy and the app's permissions before any data leaves your device, and the feature will be optional.

Children

Cerberus is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and your data

Because your data never reaches us, we hold nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can erase your vault at any time from Settings, Vault, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Erasing is permanent.

Security

We use modern, well-reviewed cryptography and design the app to keep your data on your device, but no software is perfectly secure. A strong master password, a screen lock, an up-to-date device and caution with unknown apps all matter. See Security and data handling for details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will be announced in the app or the store listing.
