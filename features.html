Features

Everything Cerberus does, in plain words. Features marked PRO come with the one-time Pro purchase. Everything else is free, with no ads. The beta download shows the Pro features but keeps them locked.

Your vault

Many item types

Logins, cards, identities, contacts, bank accounts, passkeys, bookmarks, API and developer secrets, crypto wallets and secret notes. Each type has fields that suit it.

Folders and favorites

Sort items into folders and star the ones you use most. Search finds items by name and content.

Custom fields

Add your own extra fields, text or hidden, to any item.

Photos and scans

Attach pictures such as the front and back of a licence or passport to an item. They are shrunk, stripped of location data and encrypted with their own key. Pictures are for convenience and are never included in backups or sync.

Hidden vaults PRO

Extra vaults that live alongside your main one. Available with Pro.

.onion links and contact dialer PRO

Open .onion bookmarks and call saved contacts straight from the vault. Available with Pro.

Sign-in and codes

Authenticator

Six-digit two-factor codes with next-code preview, groups, colours and custom icons. Add a token from its text key or link. Cerberus does not scan QR codes.

Generator

Passwords (length, character sets, avoid look-alike characters), passphrases (word count and separator) and usernames. Output is colour-coded by character kind so it is easy to read aloud.

Autofill

Fills logins, cards and details into apps and browsers after you unlock, and offers to save new ones. Suggestions appear as a dropdown, keyboard bar or bottom sheet depending on your Android version.

Passkey provider

On Android 14 or newer Cerberus can create and use passkeys, stored as items in your encrypted vault.

FIDO2 security key mode PRO · BETA

Tap your phone on an NFC reader and it acts as a security key, using only the passkeys in your main vault. It signs only while you have armed it on screen. NFC only, no USB or Bluetooth. Hidden vaults never act as a key.

Protection

Master password

Stretched with Argon2id tuned to your phone, with a strength rating as you choose it. Wrong tries slow down, but never erase anything.

PIN and fingerprint

Optional shortcuts. A PIN is bound to a key in your phone's secure hardware and five wrong tries remove it. The master password is asked again regularly and before sensitive actions.

Recovery phrase and image

A 20-word phrase plus a recovery image (a picture with a secret hidden in it) get you back in if you forget your master password. Both are needed together, and a built-in test checks them without changing anything.

Screen and clipboard protection

Screenshots and recent-app previews are blocked unless you allow them. Copied values are flagged sensitive and cleared after a delay you choose.

No account, no internet

There is no sign-up, no analytics, no ads and no internet permission. Everything stays on your device.

Backup, sync and moving

Encrypted backups

Make a .cerb file protected by a backup password, and optionally also by your recovery phrase. If you sign in with a PIN or fingerprint, a backup can still be made, but it then always needs your phrase too. Backups hold records only, not attached pictures.

Import and export

Import from Cerberus files, Bitwarden JSON and login CSV files from Chrome, Firefox, LastPass, 1Password and similar. Export as CSV, JSON or an encrypted backup. Plain exports are not encrypted, and the app warns you.

Personal sync PRO

Keep your devices in step through a folder you control, such as Syncthing, Nextcloud or a USB share. Passwords, items and authenticator tokens sync; pictures and hidden vaults do not. No Cerberus server is involved.

Look and feel

Themes and colours

Light, dark or follow the system, an accent colour of your choice, nine ready-made looks and exact hex colour entry.

Built-in help and legal

The user manual and the legal documents are reachable from inside the app.
